Fit the lizards
into the frame

A simple but tricky tangram puzzle for your spare time.

Lizard Out isn't about complex rules. You just have a bunch of colorful lizards and a shape that needs to be filled. Drag them around, find the right spot, and clear the board. It’s harder than it looks but very satisfying once you get it right.

HOW IT WORKS

The Goal
You get a set of unique lizard pieces. Your job is to fit every single one into the grid.
Movement
Just tap and drag. You can’t overlap pieces, and there are no gaps allowed.
Progression
Once the frame is full, you move to the next level. If you're stuck, use a hint to see where that one annoying piece actually goes.

FAQ

  • Does it cost anything?
    No, the game is free to download. There are optional ads, but you can play through all the levels without paying. We wanted to keep the core experience accessible for everyone.
  • Can I play without the internet?
    Yes, that's the best part. All the puzzles are stored in the app, so you don't need a connection to play. It only needs internet if you want to update the game or sync progress.
  • What if I can’t solve a level?
    Don’t worry, every puzzle has a solution. Try removing all pieces and starting from a different corner. If that fails, the hint button will show you the exact position of one lizard to help you finish.
